# What to take from Duolingo

Written 2026-11-17. Duolingo is a habit product that happens to teach
languages. This lists the mechanics that make people come back, what each one
does to a learner, and how it maps onto Miva Cliq. It also lists what not to
copy.

## Why it works

Three things, in this order:

1. **The session is tiny.** A lesson is three to five minutes. Starting costs
   nothing, so people start.
2. **Stopping costs something.** A streak, a league place, a daily quest half
   done. People return to avoid losing what they have.
3. **Other people can see.** Leagues, friend streaks and share cards turn a
   private chore into a public score.

Everything below serves one of those three.

## Mechanics to take

| Mechanic | What it does | Our version | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bite-size lesson** | Removes the reason not to start | Five questions, one tap each, under two minutes | 3 |
| **Instant feedback** | Right or wrong within a second, with the reason | Show the correct option and a one-line why | 3 |
| **Placement test** | Starts you at your level, not at zero | We read level and courses from the Miva login. No test needed | 1 |
| **The path** | One obvious next thing to do | Course, then week 1, week 2, in order. Next week unlocks as the semester reaches it | 2 |
| **XP** | A single number for effort | 10 per correct answer, bonus for a perfect set | 4 |
| **Streak** | The main reason people return daily | One practice set a day keeps it. Shown at the top of every page | 4 |
| **Streak freeze** | Forgives one bad day so a slip is not the end | One free a week, more bought with credits | 4 |
| **Weekly league** | Competition you can actually win | About 30 students of the same level, top 5 up, bottom 5 down, resets Sunday | 4 |
| **Daily quests** | A small goal with a finish line | "Answer 10 questions", "Get 5 in a row", "Upvote a gist" | 4 |
| **Practise your mistakes** | Spaced repetition without the jargon | Missed questions return after 1, 3 and 7 days | 3 |
| **Mastery levels** | Makes a finished unit worth revisiting | The exam-ready meter per course | 4 |
| **Milestone cards** | Free marketing, made by proud users | Top of league, 7-day streak, course 80 percent ready | 4 |
| **Friend streaks and quests** | A second person who notices if you stop | Class streak for a level: everyone practises, everyone gets bonus XP | later |
| **Timed challenge** | A burst of intensity for the keen | 60-second rapid round before exams | later |
| **Year in review** | A yearly reason to share | End-of-semester summary card | later |
| **Reminders with a voice** | Brings people back; the tone is the brand | Short, funny, Nigerian-student voice. One a day at most | 6 |
| **A mascot with moods** | Gives the app a face that reacts to you | Worth it only once the core loop holds | mobile |
| **Celebration** | Sound and motion at the finish | Confetti and a count-up on set completion | 4 |

## The order matters

Duolingo built streaks before leagues and leagues before friends. Streaks
need only one person. Leagues need thirty. Friend features need a network.
We follow the same order because early on we will not have the people for
the social features to feel alive.

## What not to copy

**Hearts.** Losing a life for each mistake punishes exactly the students who
most need practice. Duolingo itself has moved away from it. Mistakes here
should cost nothing and come back later as revision.

**Taking away what was earned.** Duolingo never deducts gems for a missed
day. The loss is the streak and the league place. We should do the same.
Deducting credits a student earned will read as the app stealing from them.

**Guilt as the main channel.** The passive-aggressive reminder is famous
because it is occasional. More than one notification a day and students
mute us for good.

**Pay to win.** If money buys league position, the league means nothing.
Credits buy AI generation and streak freezes, never XP.

**One global leaderboard.** It is won by the same few people and ignored by
the rest.

## Things Duolingo does not have that we do

- **A deadline everyone shares.** Exams. The exam-ready meter and a
  countdown give urgency that a language learner never feels.
- **A real class.** Students already know each other and already share a
  WhatsApp group. Our leagues are their actual classmates.
- **Content that writes itself.** The curriculum comes from the LMS and the
  question bank grows with use.

## The first version of each rule

Simple enough to build in a week and change later.

- XP: 10 per correct answer, 5 bonus for a perfect set of five.
- Streak: one completed set per calendar day in Lagos time.
- Freeze: one free each Monday, held until used, maximum two.
- League: grouped by level, about 30 per group, formed Monday morning.
- Promotion: top 5 go up, bottom 5 go down, the rest stay.
- Exam ready: share of a course's questions answered correctly at least
  once in the last 14 days.
